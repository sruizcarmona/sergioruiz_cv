At Huxley, we are looking for a Data Engineer / Analytics Engineer to join one of our clients in Barcelona.

Project Overview
You will design and build end-to-end data solutions in a greenfield environment, enabling real-time and batch processing. You will transform data into actionable insights through dashboards and data products, acting as a bridge between technical and business teams.

Main responsibilities
Design, build and maintain end-to-end data pipelines and data products.
Develop ingestion and transformation workflows using Python, Spark and SQL.
Build and evolve data models oriented to analytics and business consumption.
Design and implement dashboards and reporting solutions, ensuring usability and business value.
Work with event-driven / near real-time architectures (Solace-based ingestion).
Integrate data from APIs, streaming devices and cloud systems.
Collaborate closely with business stakeholders to translate requirements into data visualisations and reporting applications.
Ensure data quality, observability, governance and reliability across the full data lifecycle.
Contribute to architecture decisions in a greenfield environment.
Work with platform and cloud teams on infrastructure considerations (Azure).
Promote best practices in data engineering, visualisation and delivery.
Requirements (Must-have)
* Strong experience as Analytics Engineer / Data Engineer, with focus on end-to-end data delivery.
* Strong experience in cloud data environments, ideally Microsoft Azure.
* Hands-on experience with Databricks, Spark / PySpark and SQL.
* Strong experience building data pipelines in greenfield or highly evolving environments.
* Experience with event-driven / streaming architectures (near real-time data processing, Solace or similar).
* Experience working with cloud databases and data lakes such as Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Synapse or Databricks Lakehouse.
* Strong ability to transform data into business-oriented analytics products (dashboards, reporting layers, semantic models).
* Experience working with data visualisation tools such as Power BI, Tableau, Looker or similar.
* Strong SQL proficiency (modelling, transformations, performance).
* Ability to work across the full stack: ingestion  transformation  modelling  visualisation.
* Fluent in English and Spanish.

Nice to have
* Experience building dashboards or data applications end-to-end
* UX/UI awareness for data visualisation
* Experience with streaming tools (Kafka, Azure Event Hub, NATS)
* Experience in healthcare / regulated environments

Conditions
* Start date: ASAP
* Location: Barcelona (Hybrid)
* Full-time

Let op: vacaturefraude

Helaas komt vacaturefraude steeds vaker voor. We waarschuwen je voor mogelijke misleiding:
* Wij zullen nooit via WhatsApp of in een videogesprek vragen om jouw persoonlijke gegevens (zoals een kopie van je ID, bankgegevens of BSN).
* Twijfel je over de echtheid van een vacature of contactpersoon? Neem dan altijd rechtstreeks contact met ons op via de officië contactgegevens op onze website.

Important: job fraud

Unfortunately, job fraud is becoming more common. Beware of such scams:
* We will never ask for personal information (such as a copy of your ID, bank details, or social security number) via WhatsApp or during a video call.
* If you're unsure whether a vacancy or contact person is legitimate, please reach out to us directly using the official contact details on our website.

Overview:

We are looking for a Data Analyst to support fast-paced AI-driven initiatives, such as digital assistants, voice bots and other next-gen support tools. These roles combine a strong foundation in Data Analytics (70%) with an affinity for Data Science (30%) and will help in defining success metrics, improving data quality, reporting, and generating insights that influence strategic decisions. The ideal candidate brings a deep curiosity for user behavior, proficiency in product analytics, and a customer-first mindset that drives actionable insights.

Key Responsibilities:

Provide accurate and relevant data analysis to support informed business decisions.
Determine and prioritize the data requirements necessary to address business challenges and objectives.
Source, evaluate, and integrate new data sources to enhance analysis and insights.
Analysis, Insights gathering and recommendations for actions
Ensure that the data used for analysis is accurate, complete, and readily accessible.
Create clear and impactful visualizations, dashboards, and reports to communicate data insights effectively
Present and explain data findings to stakeholders in a way that is understandable and actionable.
Monitor and report on key performance indicators (KPIs) to track progress against business goals
Design and execute A/B tests and apply statistical methods to support data-driven decision-making.
